What you can do with it

See what matters

Every problem ranked by what it costs you.

  • Catalog health: a score, ranked priorities and the money at stake each month.
  • Analytics: prices, margins, stock and sales. Click a bar to see its products.
  • Product page card: each product's problems on its page in your admin.

Fix it fast

One product or hundreds, checked before saving.

  • Quick fixes: one click, right from the list of problems.
  • Bulk price fixes: raise prices to your target margin, with a preview.
  • Description editor: template, live checklist and preview.
  • Excel import and export: edit in Excel, review every change.

Stay on top of it

The app keeps watch and tells you when to act.

  • Scheduled scans: every day or week, at a time you choose.
  • Alerts: by email through Shopify Flow, plus a weekly summary.
  • History and undo: every change logged with its old value.

FAQ

Questions merchants ask

Something else? Email info@shoplyapps.com.

How much does it cost?

Shoply: Item Scan & Analytics is free to install, and every feature is included. If it helps your store, you can leave an optional tip.

Will the app change my products?

Only when you ask it to. A scan looks for problems and lists them for you; it doesn't edit anything. Bulk changes and Excel imports show you every change before it's saved, and every change can be undone.

When can I install it?

It's coming soon to the Shopify App Store. Email info@shoplyapps.com and we'll let you know when it's live.

How do I get alerts by email?

The app sends its alerts to Shopify Flow. Add Flow's free "Send internal email" action, and they arrive in your inbox.

Does it share my store's data?

No. The app works only with Shopify's own APIs and doesn't send your data to outside services. Our privacy policy explains what we store and why.
