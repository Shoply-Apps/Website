How it works

From install to a healthier store in minutes

Install

Add Item Scan & Analytics to your store from Shopify. It opens from Apps in your Shopify admin.

Scan

One scan checks every product. It only reads your catalog and changes nothing.

Fix what matters first

Work through your priorities, starting with what costs you money. Every change the app makes can be undone.

FAQ

Questions merchants ask

How much does Item Scan & Analytics cost?

It's free to install, and every feature is included at no charge. If it helps your store, you can leave an optional tip.

Will the app change my products?

Not on its own. A scan only reads your catalog. The app changes a product only when you ask it to, you check bulk changes and Excel imports before they're saved, and every change can be undone.

When can I install it?

Item Scan & Analytics is coming soon to the Shopify App Store. Email us at info@shoplyapps.com and we'll let you know when it's live.

How do I get alerts by email?

The app sends its alerts to Shopify Flow. Add Flow's free "Send internal email" action, and the alerts arrive in your inbox.

Does it share my store's data?

No. The app works only with Shopify's own APIs and doesn't send your data to outside services. Our privacy policy explains exactly what we store and why.

Who is behind Shoply Apps?

Shoply Apps was founded by Ignas Pilybas and Romas Gabdrafikas in Lithuania. We build apps that help Shopify merchants run healthier stores.
