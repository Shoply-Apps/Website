What you can do with it

See what matters

Every problem ranked by what it costs you.

  • Catalog health: a score, ranked priorities and the money at stake each month.
  • Analytics: prices, margins, stock and sales. Click a bar to see its products.
  • Product page card: each product's problems on its page in your admin.

Fix it fast

One product or hundreds, checked before saving.

  • Quick fixes: one click, right from the list of problems.
  • Bulk price fixes: raise prices to your target margin, with a preview.
  • Description editor: template, live checklist and preview.
  • Excel import and export: edit in Excel, review every change.

Stay on top of it

The app keeps watch and tells you when to act.

  • Scheduled scans: every day or week, at a time you choose.
  • Alerts: by email through Shopify Flow, plus a weekly summary.
  • History and undo: every change logged with its old value.
How it works

Install

Add the app from Shopify. It opens from Apps in your admin.

Scan

One scan checks every product for 27 problems and ranks them by what they cost you.

Fix what matters first

Start with what costs you money. Every change can be undone.

FAQ

Questions merchants ask

Something else? Email info@shoplyapps.com.

Which apps do you offer?

Our first app, Shoply: Item Scan & Analytics, is coming soon to the Shopify App Store. More apps are in the works, and we'd love to hear what would help your store.

How much do your apps cost?

Shoply: Item Scan & Analytics is free to install at launch. If we add paid plans later, we'll tell you well in advance, and Shopify always asks for your approval before any charge.

Will your apps change my store?

Only when you ask them to. Item Scan & Analytics, for example, looks for problems and lists them for you; it edits a product only when you choose a fix, shows bulk changes before they're saved, and lets you undo every change.

Do you share my store's data?

No. Our apps work only with Shopify's own APIs and don't send your data to outside services. Our privacy policy explains what we store and why.

How can I reach you?

Email info@shoplyapps.com with questions, feedback or ideas for new apps.
