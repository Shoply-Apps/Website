Shopify product image sizes: dimensions, file size and consistency

By the Shoply Apps team · 20 October 2026 · 9 min read

Key takeaways

  • A square image of about 2048 × 2048 px is a widely recommended Shopify product image size: sharp when zoomed and on high-resolution screens.
  • Use one aspect ratio for every product image, either square 1:1 or a portrait ratio such as 4:5, so collection grids line up.
  • Resize and compress before uploading; as a rule of thumb, a 2048 px product photo can often be 200–500 KB with no visible quality loss.
  • Use JPEG for photos and PNG for graphics needing transparency, with descriptive hyphenated file names and alt text on every image.
  • Give each colour variant its own linked image, and keep the main image free of promotional text for Google Shopping.

The best Shopify product image size for most stores is a square image of about 2048 × 2048 pixels, compressed to a sensible file size and used at the same aspect ratio across every product. That combination looks sharp when shoppers zoom, stays crisp on high-resolution phone screens and keeps your collection pages tidy. This guide covers dimensions, aspect ratio, file size, formats, file names, alt text and the other images your store needs, with a checklist at the end.

What is the best Shopify product image size?

A square image of around 2048 × 2048 px is a widely recommended choice for Shopify product images. Shopify's help center has recommended 2048 × 2048 px for square product images; check its current guidance for maximum file and pixel sizes, as those limits can change.

Why that size works:

  • Zoom. Many themes let shoppers zoom in or open a full-size view. A small image turns blurry the moment it is enlarged.
  • High-resolution screens. Modern phones and laptops pack two or three device pixels into each CSS pixel, so an image displayed 800 px wide can need 1600 px or more of real detail to look sharp.
  • Headroom without waste. Going far beyond 2048 px adds file weight that most shoppers never see.

Which aspect ratio should you use for Shopify product images?

Pick one aspect ratio and use it for every product image. Aspect ratio is the shape of the image, written as width to height: 1:1 is square, 4:5 is slightly taller than wide. Either works; consistency matters more than the choice itself.

  • Square (1:1), for example 2048 × 2048 px, is the safe default. It suits most products and fits neatly in most theme grids.
  • Portrait (4:5), for example 1600 × 2000 px, suits clothing, bottles and other tall products.

When images in the same collection have different shapes, the grid looks uneven. Some themes show each image at its own shape, so cards end up different heights. Others crop images to fit a fixed frame, which can cut off the top of a bottle or the toe of a shoe, or add blank bars around the image (called letterboxing). Many themes let you choose how images are cropped in collection grids, but that setting cannot rescue a catalogue of mixed shapes. The fix is to crop every image to the same ratio before you upload.

How big should Shopify image files be?

Keep each product image as small as you can without visible loss of quality. As a rough rule of thumb, a 2048 × 2048 px product photo saved as a well-compressed JPEG often lands somewhere between 200 KB and 500 KB. These are not Shopify limits, just a sensible target; a detailed photo will be heavier than a plain one.

File size is the amount of storage the image takes up, in kilobytes (KB) or megabytes (MB). A photo straight from a modern phone or camera can be several megabytes and much larger in pixels than you need. Two steps fix most of that:

  1. Resize the image to your chosen dimensions, for example 2048 × 2048 px.
  2. Compress it with an image editor or an online compression tool, then compare it with the original at full size. If you cannot see a difference, keep the smaller file.

Shopify's CDN (content delivery network, the servers that deliver your images to shoppers) generally creates resized copies of each image and serves modern formats such as WebP to browsers that support them. That helps a lot, but it works from your original, so a lighter, well-prepared original is still the best starting point. Faster pages matter: slow-loading product pages are a common reason shoppers give up, and image weight is one of the easiest things to control. How much page weight can compression save? A worked example

Here is an example product page with 8 images. The numbers are illustrative, not measurements from a real store.

VersionPer image8 images
Straight from the camera (6000 × 4000 px)4.0 MB32.0 MB
Resized to 2048 × 2048 px, light compression900 KB7.2 MB
Resized and well compressed350 KB2.8 MB

Saving = 32.0 MB − 2.8 MB = 29.2 MB
Reduction = 29.2 ÷ 32.0 = about 91%

Shoppers will not normally download all 32 MB at once, because Shopify's CDN generally serves smaller resized copies in grids and thumbnails. But full-size views such as zoom and lightbox galleries can request much larger versions, and on a slow mobile connection the difference between a 4 MB and a 350 KB image is noticeable.

Which image format should you use: JPEG, PNG or WebP?

Use JPEG for product photos, PNG for graphics that need a transparent background or sharp edges, and WebP if your editing tools export it well. Shopify accepts common formats including JPEG, PNG and WebP; check its help center for the full current list.

FormatBest forWatch out for
JPEGPhotos of products and lifestyle shotsToo much compression causes blocky artefacts
PNGLogos, icons and graphics with transparency or textPhotos saved as PNG are often far heavier than JPEG
WebPPhotos and graphics at smaller file sizesSome older editing tools and other channels handle it less well

How should you name image files before uploading?

Give each file a short, descriptive name in lowercase with hyphens between words, before you upload it. For example, sage-stoneware-mug-350ml.jpg is far more useful than IMG_4821.jpg.

  • Describe what is in the image: product, colour, size or angle (sage-stoneware-mug-350ml-side.jpg).
  • Use hyphens, not spaces or underscores.

Descriptive names make your files easier to manage and give search engines a small extra clue about the image. Rename files on your computer first, so each image goes in with the right name from the start.

How do you write alt text for product images?

Alt text is a short written description of an image, read aloud by screen readers and used by search engines to understand the picture. In Shopify you can add it to each image in the product's media section.

Describe what the image actually shows, in plain words, in about one sentence: "Sage green stoneware mug, 350 ml, shown from the side with a matte glaze." Do not start with "image of", and do not repeat the same text on every image of the product. For titles, descriptions and alt text together, follow our Shopify product page SEO checklist.

How should you set up variant images?

Upload at least one image for each colour or style and link it to that variant, so the main image changes when a shopper picks an option. In Shopify, each variant can be assigned an image from the product's media.

  • Shoot every colour from the same angle, with the same lighting and framing, so switching variants feels seamless.
  • Use the same aspect ratio and dimensions for every variant image.
  • Name files by variant (sage-stoneware-mug-350ml-blue.jpg) and write alt text that mentions the colour.

What sizes do collection banners, logos and other store images need?

Sizes for images outside the product page depend on your theme and the section you put them in, so check your theme's documentation or the hints shown in the theme editor.

Image typeSuggested approach
Product imagesSquare around 2048 × 2048 px (or one consistent portrait ratio such as 4:5), compressed, descriptive file name and alt text
Variant imagesSame ratio and dimensions as main images, one per colour, linked to the variant
Collection bannersWide image; check the theme's guidance; keep the subject away from edges that may be cropped on mobile
Homepage heroLarge, wide image; check the theme's guidance; compress well as it loads first; avoid important text baked into the image
LogoPNG or SVG with a transparent background; upload at about twice the width it displays at for sharpness
FaviconSimple, square version of your logo that still reads at a tiny size; follow the size shown in the theme editor
Blog imagesOne consistent ratio for featured images; compress; add alt text

Wide banners are often cropped differently on phones than on desktops. After uploading, preview every banner on a phone and adjust the focal point if your theme offers one.

What images does Google Shopping need?

Google Shopping needs a main image that shows the product clearly, without promotional overlays such as "SALE" text, watermarks, logos added on top or borders. Google Merchant Center can disapprove products whose images break its rules, so check its current image requirements before you sync your catalogue.

A plain or light background, the whole product in frame and sharp focus are a safe choice for your first image. Images are only one part of a healthy product feed; see Google Shopping on Shopify: GTIN, brand and product category for the rest.

Shopify product image checklist

Run through this before uploading new products, and once in a while across your whole catalogue:

  1. Every product has at least one image.
  2. All product images use the same aspect ratio (square 1:1 or one portrait ratio).
  3. Images are around 2048 px on the longest side, or close to it.
  4. Files are compressed with no visible quality loss.
  5. JPEG for photos, PNG for graphics needing transparency.
  6. File names are descriptive, lowercase and hyphenated.
  7. Every image has alt text that describes what it shows.
  8. Each colour or style variant has its own linked image.
  9. The first image shows the product clearly, with no promotional text or watermark.
  10. Banners and hero images have been previewed on a phone.

FAQ

What size should Shopify product images be?

A square image of around 2048 × 2048 pixels is a widely recommended choice. It stays sharp when shoppers zoom and on high-resolution screens. Check Shopify's help center for its current maximum file and pixel sizes.

Should Shopify product images be square?

Square 1:1 images are the safest default because they fit most theme grids, but a portrait ratio such as 4:5 also works well for tall products. What matters most is using the same ratio for every product image.

Does Shopify compress images automatically?

Shopify's CDN generally creates resized copies of your images and serves modern formats such as WebP to browsers that support them. It still works from your original, so resizing and compressing before upload gives the best results.

Can I use different image sizes for different products?

You can, but collection pages will look uneven, and some themes will crop or add blank bars to images that do not match. Crop every product image to the same aspect ratio before uploading.
