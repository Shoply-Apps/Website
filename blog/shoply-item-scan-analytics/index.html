Shoply: Item Scan & Analytics: what it does and why we built it

By the Shoply Apps team · 20 October 2026 · 9 min read

Key takeaways

  • Shoply: Item Scan & Analytics is a Shopify catalog audit app that checks every product for 27 problems across pricing and margin, stock, and descriptions and content.
  • It ranks every problem by what it costs you per month, so you fix the expensive ones first, and gives your catalog a health score.
  • Fixes are one click or in bulk with a preview; it only changes a product when you choose a fix, and every change can be undone.
  • It uses only Shopify's own APIs, runs in Frankfurt, Germany, doesn't store customers' personal data, and deletes your store's data when you uninstall.
  • It is coming soon to the Shopify App Store and will be free to install at launch.

Shoply Item Scan & Analytics is a Shopify app, made by us at Shoply Apps, that scans every product in your store for 27 common problems and ranks them by the money at stake each month. It works inside your Shopify admin, suggests fixes you can apply in one click or in bulk, and never changes a product unless you choose to. This article explains what it checks, how the ranking works, how it handles your data, and what it deliberately doesn't do.

Why did we build a Shopify catalog audit app?

We built it because small catalog mistakes quietly cost money, and they pile up as a store grows. A variant priced below its cost, a best-seller that shows as in stock when it isn't, a product with no description: each one is easy to fix once you see it, and easy to miss among hundreds of products.

Shoply Apps was founded in Lithuania by Ignas Pilybas and Romas Gabdrafikas. Our motivation was simple: the information you need to spot these problems already sits in your Shopify admin, but it is spread across product pages, inventory screens and order history. Checking it by hand means exporting spreadsheets and working through a list like our monthly catalog health checklist. That works, but most store owners don't have time to do it every month, and the list rarely tells you which problem to fix first.

So we wanted one tool that does the checking for you and answers a single question: which problems are costing me the most right now?

What does Shoply Item Scan & Analytics check?

It checks every product for 27 problems in three categories: pricing and margin (7 checks), stock (8 checks), and descriptions and content (12 checks). Each check flags a specific, fixable issue. A variant is one version of a product, such as a size or colour, with its own price, cost and stock.

How does it rank problems by money at stake?

It puts a monthly money figure on each problem and sorts the list by that figure, so the most expensive problems sit at the top. A long list of issues is hard to act on; a list ordered by cost tells you where to start.

The idea is easiest to see with an example. The numbers below are made up for illustration, and the app's exact internal calculation is not shown here.

Example: a variant priced $2 below its cost sells 40 units a month.
Money at stake ≈ $2 × 40 = $80 a month

Compare that with a product missing alt text on its images. It is worth fixing, but it is not losing $80 a month on every sale. With money at stake, the below-cost variant comes first.

Here is how an example scan might order three problems (illustrative numbers only):

Variant $1 under margin target, 25 sold a month$25 / monthNext
Slow seller with images missing alt textSmallWhen you have time

The app reads your orders only to total units sold and revenue per variant over the last 60 days; it doesn't store customers' personal data. Your scan results add up to a catalog health score, a ranked list of priorities and the total money at stake each month.

What else can it do besides scanning?

Beyond the scan, the app helps you see what matters, fix it quickly, and keep on top of it over time.

See what matters

  • Catalog health: a score, ranked priorities and the money at stake each month.
  • Analytics: charts of prices, margins, stock and sales; click a bar to see the products behind it.
  • Product page card: each product's problems shown on its own page in the Shopify admin.

Fix it fast

  • Quick fixes: one click from the list of problems.
  • Bulk price fixes: raise prices to your target margin, with a preview before anything is saved. If margins are new to you, our guide to pricing for a healthy profit margin explains the maths.
  • Description editor: a template, a live checklist and a preview.
  • Excel import and export: edit in a spreadsheet, then review every change before saving.

Stay on top of it

  • Scheduled scans: every day or every week, at a time you choose.
  • Alerts: email alerts through Shopify Flow, plus a weekly summary.
  • History and undo: every change is logged with its old value, so you can reverse it.

How does the app handle my store's data?

It keeps data use to what the checks need and stays inside Shopify. In short:

  • It works inside your Shopify admin and uses only Shopify's own APIs.
  • It uses no outside services or trackers.
  • The app and its database run in Frankfurt, Germany, in the EU.
  • It doesn't store your customers' personal data. It reads orders only to total units sold and revenue per variant over the last 60 days.
  • Uninstalling deletes your store's data straight away.

The full details are in our privacy policy.

How does it work?

It works in three steps:

  1. Install. The app opens from Apps in your Shopify admin.
  2. Scan. One scan checks every product for 27 problems and ranks them by cost.
  3. Fix what matters first. Work down the ranked list with quick fixes or bulk fixes, previewing changes before they are saved.

What doesn't it do?

To be clear about its limits, here is what the app is not:

  • It doesn't submit feeds to Google. It checks the product data Google Shopping relies on, but sending products to Google Merchant Center happens elsewhere.
  • It doesn't change anything without your say-so. It only changes a product when you choose a fix, bulk changes are shown before they are saved, and every change can be undone.
  • It isn't an inventory or ERP system. It flags stock problems in your Shopify catalog, but it doesn't manage purchasing, suppliers or warehouses. An ERP (enterprise resource planning) system is software for running those wider operations.

FAQ

Is Shoply: Item Scan & Analytics available yet?

Not yet. It is coming soon to the Shopify App Store and will be free to install at launch. If paid plans are added later, merchants will be told well in advance, and Shopify always asks for your approval before any charge.

Will the app change my products automatically?

No. It only changes a product when you choose a fix. Bulk changes are shown to you before they are saved, and every change is logged with its old value so it can be undone.

Does it store my customers' personal data?

No. It reads orders only to total units sold and revenue per variant over the last 60 days. The app and its database run in Frankfurt, Germany, and uninstalling deletes your store's data straight away.

Does it send my products to Google Shopping?

No. It checks the product data Google Shopping relies on, such as barcode (GTIN), brand and product category, but it does not submit feeds to Google Merchant Center.
